Before you jump in

Server Rules

A lot of things that would get you banned on a normal server are just part of the game here.

  • 1Hacked clients, X-ray, and any form of cheating result in a permanent ban.
  • 2Exploiting duplication glitches or other bugs for profit is bannable — report them instead.
  • 3Real-money trading of items, currency, or accounts outside the official store is a permanent ban.
  • 4Scamming other players in trades is allowed — verify every deal yourself before you commit to it.
  • 5Raiding, base-breaking, and PvP outside protected spawn areas are normal gameplay, not griefing.
  • 6Bots and alt accounts used to automate farming or gain an unfair edge are not allowed.
  • 7Market manipulation — auction flipping, bulk buying and reselling — is a legitimate part of the economy.
  • 8Hate speech, doxxing, and harassment are not tolerated and result in an immediate ban.
Quick start

How to Join

01

Open Minecraft

Launch Java Edition, recommended version: latest release.

02

Multiplayer → Add Server

Click "Add Server" in the multiplayer list.

03

Enter the IP Address

Type play.gloresmp.example as the server address.

04

Connect and Play

Save, double-click the server, and welcome to GloreSMP!
